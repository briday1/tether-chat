Share Home

Experimental household sync uses one shared Google Sheet as an append-only change journal. Your task list stays in tether-home; this is not Google Calendar integration.

Your local database stays on this device. Only Home tasks, categories, task items and notes sync. Work, conversations, API keys, AI instructions and notification history stay private.

Google connection

App setup

The publisher must configure a Google OAuth web client for this exact origin. This is a public client ID, never a client secret.

Use a browser-restricted Picker key, not a service-account key or OAuth client secret. These app settings are saved on this browser only.

Authorization stays in memory, not in backups. When Google access expires, reconnect to continue. Offline edits stay local.

Your household

Each person connects their own Google account. There is no separate tether account. Everyone chooses the same household Sheet in Drive.

Share household in Google Drive

Open the Sheet, choose Share, enter your partner’s email as an Editor and send Google’s invitation. They choose Join shared household here and select that same Sheet. A copied link alone does not grant access.

Additional phones and computers also choose Join and select the same Sheet. Each keeps its local copy. Do not edit, sort or delete the Journal rows manually: they hold change history, not a task list.
