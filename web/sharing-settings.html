Sync status and recovery

Sync

Sync is automatic: tether checks for changes every 30 seconds while connected and open, including when you leave Settings. Sync now is an optional immediate check or retry. Offline changes wait for your connection; reconnect Google when authorization expires.

Not connected

Disconnect keeps local tasks and existing Drive files. To stop another person’s access, change the Sheet’s sharing permissions in Google Drive.
